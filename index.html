System Integration

KGA ARIA is ready to integrate beside carrier systems through signed event contracts, canonical normalization, neutral PAS ports, and governed outbound commands. The KGA ARIA Integration Principle

KGA ARIA does not replace the core system. It assembles cross-lifecycle context, applies specialized insurance reasoning, enforces carrier authority, and prepares approved integration commands through neutral contracts and Guidewire-first adapters. The public environment uses controlled notes-only simulation; reserve, payment, cancellation, and policy mutations are activated only against an authorized carrier endpoint after mapping, security, authority, and acceptance validation.

Control Plane — Every Decision Shows Its Work

Every agent action is rendered through one envelope: identity → scope → policy gate → evidence → confidence → disposition. Behind it sits a versioned workflow registry — each crew (renewal underwriting, FNOL-to-reserve, commission review, dunning & retention, premium audit) carries a version number, an approval status, and a last-validated date, so what's running in production is always traceable to what was approved.
