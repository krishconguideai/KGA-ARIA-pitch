The KGA ARIA Integration Principle

KGA ARIA does not replace the core system. It assembles cross-lifecycle context, applies specialized insurance reasoning, enforces carrier authority, and prepares approved integration commands through neutral contracts and Guidewire-first adapters. The shared evaluation environment uses controlled notes-only simulation; reserve, payment, cancellation, and policy mutations are activated only against an authorized carrier endpoint after mapping, security, authority, and acceptance validation.

Enterprise Readiness

Built for Regulated Decisions. Production Proof in Progress.

KGA ARIA is engineered for regulated enterprise operations: tenant-scoped access, sensitive-field encryption, canonical audit bodies, hash-chained decision records, human approval continuations, a per-domain authority matrix, governed outbound commands, a cross-domain decision receipt, per-stage operating contracts, retry and reconciliation, and documented recovery design. The architecture is implemented and testable; deployment-specific scale baselines, operating evidence, and external attestations are established with each carrier environment.

🛰️

Control Plane — Every Decision Shows Its Work

Every agent action is rendered through one envelope: identity → scope → policy gate → evidence → confidence → disposition. Behind it sits a versioned workflow registry — each crew (renewal underwriting, FNOL-to-reserve, commission review, dunning & retention, premium audit) carries a version number, an approval status, and a last-validated date — alongside a per-domain authority matrix that classifies every Policy, Billing, and Claims action as permitted, referred, or human-approval-required. A single decision receipt reconstructs one account's full Policy → Billing → Claims decision trail end to end.

Identity → Scope → Policy Gate → Evidence → Confidence → DispositionVersioned Workflow RegistryPer-Domain Authority MatrixCross-Domain Decision Receipt
🧭

Human-in-the-Loop Trust & Governance

A dedicated supervisor queue holds any AI decision above a carrier-configured threshold for human review before write-back, and an audit-verify surface lets a compliance reviewer reconstruct and defend any past decision on demand. Every governed decision carries a canonical contract — accountable actor, trigger, rule applied, state transition, exception path, and required evidence — so what a decision was obligated to show is explicit, not implied. The platform is built to know when to stop — and to prove why it stopped.

Resilience — Survives a Bad Day

The governed outbound path distinguishes retryable, permanent, ambiguous, conflict, stale-context, and later-rejection outcomes instead of treating every dispatch as success.
